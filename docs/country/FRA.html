Today's brief

France faces deepening fiscal crisis as parliament rejects budget and yields approach 5%

  • France faces mounting fiscal risks and rising debt costs as government bond yields near 5%, following an average 10-year yield of 4.90% in October.
  • The French Parliament rejected the austerity budget, reportedly with zero votes, sparking fears of a fiscal crisis.
  • Nationwide strikes and protests erupted across France, including 600,000 demonstrators and strikes by teachers and students opposing proposed education cuts.
  • IMF forecasts project France's fiscal deficit at -5.4% of GDP in 2026, with government debt expected to reach 118.5% of GDP.
