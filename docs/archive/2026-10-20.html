How this brief is made

Each morning a Python pipeline pulls headlines (Google News RSS), IMF World Economic Outlook forecasts, BIS policy rates and FX rates for ten CEEMEA sovereigns. Headlines are ranked for bond relevance by a transparent keyword model. Changes since the previous brief (rate moves, FX moves over 1%, IMF revisions) are computed directly from the data. Before publishing, every figure in the text is checked against the cited source; anything that does not match is flagged ⚑ rather than removed.
